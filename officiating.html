METHODOLOGY

How this feature stays trustworthy

Source hierarchy

Primary: official GoGriz box scores for game metadata, seven-person crews and team penalty totals.

Secondary: Big Sky/NCAA officiating and rules material plus independent PBP sources for cross-checking.

Evidence: video and credible reporting for notable-call review when box-score data cannot answer the question.

Publication gate

Official totals are authoritative. Unknown yardage remains NULL. Compound and offsetting fouls are preserved. A game cannot be marked fully reconciled simply because its headline totals are known.

Notable calls use four evidence labels: clearly correct, debatable, likely incorrect, and insufficient evidence.
