DATA PHILOSOPHY

How Griz HQ handles officiating data

Source hierarchy

1. Official Montana/GoGriz box scores for game metadata, seven-person crews and team penalty totals.

2. Official conference/NCAA material for officiating structure, rules and formal replay/appeal information.

3. Independent structured PBP sources for cross-checking event details.

4. Video and journalism for evidence that cannot be established from box-score data alone.

Publication gates

Official team totals are authoritative. Event-level records must reconcile before they are treated as complete. Unknown yardage remains NULL. Compound and offsetting fouls are preserved rather than forced into invented values.

Notable-call assessments use four labels: clearly correct, debatable, likely incorrect, and insufficient evidence.
