DATA PHILOSOPHY

The rules that keep this feature honest

Source hierarchy

Primary: Montana Athletics / GoGriz for game metadata, seven-person crews, official team penalty totals and PBP.

Official context: Big Sky and NCAA materials for officiating structure, rules and formal replay information.

Independent cross-check: ESPN and StatBroadcast where available.

Evidence layer: legitimate broadcast/video and credible reporting for case-specific questions.

Publication gates

Official totals can display while event-level reconstruction is under review. Unknown yardage stays unknown. Compound and offsetting fouls are preserved rather than invented.

Notable calls use clearly correct, debatable, likely incorrect, or insufficient evidence.
